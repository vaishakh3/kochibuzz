# Kochi Buzz launch film — City Frequency

## Format

- Master: 1920 × 1080, 30fps, 40 seconds.
- Deliverable: H.264 MP4 with AAC stereo audio and a PNG poster.
- Purpose: launch post, website showcase, event projection and press sharing.
- Product proof is always the real Kochi Buzz interface. Generated photography is
  used only as editorial brand imagery, never as evidence for a specific event.

## Creative idea

Kochi already has a rhythm. Kochi Buzz makes it legible. The film begins in the
city before dawn, finds the signal, dives into the datebook, then expands from a
single date to people, work and things built here. It ends where the product
begins: the calendar.

## One visual system

- Canvas: Harbour ink `#0B0B12`; warm paper `#F5F0E7` only for product cards.
- Live signal: Chartreuse `#D7F24B`; never used as general decoration.
- Human energy: Coral `#FF6542` for pulses, people and contribution.
- Community: Lagoon `#72DCC7`; projects: lavender `#C7B4EE`.
- Headlines: Fraunces, large and editorial. Utility copy: Geist. Evidence labels:
  Geist Mono, uppercase with wide tracking.
- Motion vocabulary: signal wipe, coral pulse cut, physical card slide. No generic
  zoom transitions, glitch presets, lens flares or unrelated effects.
- Texture: one fine monochrome grain layer across the complete film.
- Image grade: cool harbour shadows, natural skin, restrained warm practicals.

## Audio direction

Original 108 BPM score in D minor: harbour-low sub pulse, warm detuned pad,
glass-like four-note signal motif, tactile electronic percussion and quiet water
noise. The arrangement grows with the product reveal, breathes for the attendance
moment, then resolves into a four-note brand mnemonic. No licensed samples.
