# ImageGen asset provenance

All four plates were generated in ImageGen’s built-in mode for this film. They
share the same documentary grade: contemporary Kochi, cool harbour shadows,
natural skin, restrained coral practicals, chartreuse edge accents, cinematic
large-format texture, and no text or logos.

## `public/art/kochi-harbour-dawn.png`

> Widescreen 16:9 cinematic editorial photograph of contemporary Kochi at blue
> hour, viewed low from the water. A public ferry crosses the foreground; Marine
> Drive’s modern waterfront and a Chinese fishing-net silhouette sit in the
> distance. Deep harbour-ink and teal shadows, a few warm windows and practical
> lights, natural monsoon atmosphere, large-format documentary detail, generous
> negative space for typography. Authentic lived-in city, not a travel postcard.
