# Kochi On Air artwork

## `kochi-on-air.webp`

- Purpose: homepage broadcast panorama and primary people-first brand artwork.
- Origin: generated specifically for Kochi Buzz with OpenAI ImageGen on
  2026-08-21; no stock image or real-event photograph was used.
- Art direction: editorial screen-print / risograph collage showing clearly
  illustrated people talking, recording community radio, prototyping, meeting
  and using a ferry in a contemporary Kochi setting. Local context includes
  backwaters, Chinese fishing-net geometry, metro, port cranes, foliage and
  monsoon cloud forms.
- Palette: harbour ink, warm paper, signal chartreuse, coral, lagoon and
  lavender from the Kochi Buzz brand system.
- Integrity constraint: no identifiable real people, event attendance claim,
  words, logos or fabricated documentary context.
- Production: generated at 1586×992, encoded as an 1800×1127 WebP at quality 82
  for responsive full-bleed delivery.

## `kochi-on-air-social.jpg`

- Purpose: text-safe background for the dynamic 1200×630 social preview.
- Origin: a single ImageGen recomposition of the master artwork on 2026-08-21,
  keeping people on the right and harbour-ink negative space on the left.
- Integrity constraints: identical to the master artwork; no generated text is
  embedded in the raster. Social titles remain real HTML rendered by the OG
  image route.
- Production: encoded as a 1600×840 JPEG at quality 84 because the OG image
  renderer does not accept WebP input.

## `kochi-community-night.webp`

- Purpose: editorial panorama for the homepage community directory.
- Origin: generated specifically for Kochi Buzz with OpenAI ImageGen on
  2026-08-21, using the master broadcast artwork as a style reference only.
- Art direction: a fictional, contemporary Kochi community evening with people
  welcoming a newcomer, sharing a laptop, sketching ideas, building electronics
  and talking over chai. Rain, Metro, palms and backwater light locate the
  illustration without claiming to document a real gathering.
- Integrity constraint: the people are fictional and are not presented as
  members of the named communities listed beside the artwork. No words, logos,
  identifiable real people or fabricated event documentation appear in it.
- Production: generated at 1672×941 and encoded as WebP at quality 84.
